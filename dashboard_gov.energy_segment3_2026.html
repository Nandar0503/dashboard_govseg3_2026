Data Coverage Note

Response Rate and First Contact Resolution (FCR) are not shown here: the source traffic register has no reliable, consistently-calculated field for either metric for the digital channel (the closest column is a near-constant flag, not a real ratio), so per the same rule used everywhere else on this dashboard they are left out rather than modeled. Likewise, the source data does not separate digital volume into “case in” vs “case out” — only total interactions received are tracked, shown here with the real per-channel breakdown instead. Channels that are a real column in the source register but recorded zero volume everywhere in this workbook (Video Call, SMS, Aplikasi, Hangout, Apps) are omitted from the chart/list for the periods where they are zero, not because they were removed as data.
